# End-to-end trace: the generated fabric versus its intent (chapter 5)

Trace date: 2026-08-26. Lab: `ch05` (`bgpbook-ch05`), Cumulus Linux VX 5.12.0,
generated by `gen_topo.py` from `spec.yml` (2 nodes, 4 GPUs per node, 2
spines: 4 rail leafs, 16 links, 32 intent endpoints). Audit
`ch05_audit_20260826_205304` fully green at collection time. Raw outputs in
`raw/`.

## 1. The fabric came up as designed

All 8 eBGP sessions Established (`raw/rail1_summary.txt`,
`raw/spine1_summary.txt`): each rail leaf sees 2 spines, each spine sees 4
rail leafs, ASNs 65101 to 65104 below, 65100 on top. Node1 reached node2 on
every rail's subnet (`raw/rail1_ping.txt` for rail 1; the audit covers all
four).

## 2. Spine count is ECMP width

From rail1, another rail's subnet has exactly two starred next hops, one per
spine, over the RFC 8950 link-local encoding (`raw/rail1_ecmp.txt`):

```
* fe80::a8c1:abff:fe08:1d6b, via swp3, weight 1
* fe80::a8c1:abff:feb5:78e7, via swp4, weight 1
```

## 3. What the verifier consumes: LLDP on the wire

tcpdump on node1's eth1 caught both directions of the announcement cycle
(`raw/node1_lldp_wire.txt`): node1 advertising `System Name: node1, Port
Description: eth1`, and rail1 answering `System Name: rail1, Port ID: swp1`
with `MTU size 9216` in its 802.3 TLV. `verify_lldp.sh` collects these
announcements from every switch and diffs them against `intent.json`.

## 4. Cabling versus intent: 24 endpoints, zero mismatches

`raw/verify.txt`: every switch-side port checked against `intent.json`, all
OK, verdict `CABLING MATCHES INTENT`. This is the healed fabric; the same
check on the deliberately miscabled topology is recorded separately in
`../break5a/`.

## 5. The break-5a story (../break5a/)

`gen_topo.py --swap-rails 2 3` deployed the node-side cabling of rails 2 and
3 swapped while `intent.json` kept the design:

- `verify_output.txt`: exactly 4 MISMATCH lines naming the swapped ports
  (rail2/rail3 swp1 and swp2, each seeing the other rail's node NIC).
- `ping_output.txt`: all four per-rail node1-to-node2 pings still PASS,
  because both nodes were swapped symmetrically and land in the same L2
  domain on the wrong leaf. The dataplane looks healthy.
- `gateway_output.txt`: rails 2 and 3 cannot reach their own gateways
  (172.31.2.1 and 172.31.3.1 FAIL), because the swapped NICs' frames arrive
  on a leaf hosting a different rail's subnet.

The miscable that survives a smoke test, caught by the LLDP-versus-intent
diff in one command.
